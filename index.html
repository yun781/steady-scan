Frequently asked questions

How does auto capture work?

Point your camera at a page. When the edges are detected, the page is steady, and the camera is in focus, the shutter counts down 3‑2‑1 and captures the page for you. You can switch to manual capture with the Auto button at the top of the camera.

Can I use the iPhone’s built‑in document scanner instead?

Yes. Go to Settings › Scanning › Document Capture and choose Built‑in iOS Scanner.

How do I scan a QR code or barcode?

Open the Scan tab and switch to QR & Barcode. Hold the code inside the frame for a moment. Your scans are saved under Library › QR History.

What’s included for free?

Document scanning (with no page limits), PDF and image export, and QR and barcode scanning are free. Free exports include a small “Scanned with Steady Scan” label in the corner.

What does Pro include?

Exports without the watermark, text extraction (OCR), and Text mode in the camera.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Steady Scan, and tap Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my documents stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Deleting the app deletes your documents, so export anything you want to keep first.
