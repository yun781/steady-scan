Steady Scan

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The short version: Steady Scan does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Everything you scan stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What the app does with your data

Camera

Steady Scan uses your camera to scan documents and read QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device and are never sent to us or to any server.

Photos

When you import from Photos, you choose which images to share with the app using the system photo picker. The app does not have access to the rest of your library. If you choose Save Image when exporting, the app adds that image to your photo library, and nothing else.

Documents and scan history

Your scanned documents, their names, and your QR and barcode history are stored only inside the app on your device. They are deleted when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, that backup may include the app’s data, as controlled by your device settings and Apple’s policies.

Text recognition

Text extraction (OCR), automatic document naming, and page detection run entirely on your device using Apple’s built-in frameworks. No text or images leave your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether you have an active subscription so it can unlock Pro features.

Links from QR codes

When you open a website, phone number, email address, or other link found in a QR code, it opens in the corresponding app on your device. Those websites and services have their own privacy policies.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, or location, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children’s privacy

Steady Scan does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
